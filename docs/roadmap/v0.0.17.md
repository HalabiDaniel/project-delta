# paperwars.io: Version 0.0.17 Development Plan

**Development stage:** Private Playable Alpha
**Release type:** Progression feature (cosmetic only)
**Release theme:** Player Ranks: a long climb from Blank Page to Head of the Class, with insignia, profile cards and aura to show for it
**Builds on:** [v0.0.15](v0.0.15.md) (HUD and UI revamp) and [v0.0.16](v0.0.16.md) (flight maneuvers)
**Followed by:** [v0.0.18](v0.0.18.md) (economy and content depth)

Part of the [roadmap to v0.1.0](README.md). Added on 2026-11-06 (see
[the decisions](README.md#decisions-of-2026-11-06)).

---

## Overview

Players already keep their base, planes, paper and progress across sessions (v0.0.12), but
nothing shows how long or how well someone has played. A player in their hundredth session looks
exactly like one in their first.

Version 0.0.17 adds **player ranks**. Every player earns **Merit** by playing and climbs a ladder
of ranks, each with levels (I, II, III and for the star ranks IV), like soldiers moving up through
the ranks. The names are not military. They come from the game's world: a classroom, paper and
folding. A new player is a *Blank Page*; the very top is *Head of the Class*.

**Ranks are purely cosmetic.** A rank never changes a plane, a price, a reward, a matchmaking
decision or anything else about play. What it gives is **status and aura**: an insignia next to
the player's name, a profile card that gets fancier with every tier, titles, a desk pennant, and
from the middle ranks on, plane effects that everyone in the session can see.

The release has six central objectives:

1. A rank ladder of 15 ranks (48 levels), plus endless Honor Roll stars after the top, with
   creative, game-themed names.
2. Merit: earned on the server from real play, recorded through an idempotent ledger, never lost
   and never spent.
3. Rank rewards that are cosmetic only: insignia, profile cards, titles, nameplates, pennants,
   trails, spawn effects and auras.
4. A **profile card** for every player, visible in the scoreboard, on desk nameplates, after a
   shoot-down and on the session results.
5. A promotion moment worth waiting for, which never interrupts a fight.
6. Anti-farming rules so a rank means time and skill, not a trick.

Preserve:

- The persistence layer and the ledger from v0.0.10 and v0.0.12; Merit uses the same patterns.
- The v0.0.15 design system: every card, badge and screen is built from its components.
- Readability: friend and foe colours, silhouettes and stealth (v0.0.18) must never be affected by
  rank effects.
- Every existing feature and its tests.

**A rank is not a skill rating.** It measures time and achievement and only goes up. Competitive
ladders and ranked play are a separate, later idea (after v0.1.0) and are not part of this
version. Matchmaking (v0.0.26) does not use player ranks.

## 1. The Rank Ladder

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** A ladder that is quick to start climbing, satisfying in the middle, and takes a long time
to finish.

The levels show in the insignia itself (more chevrons, bars, stars, a brighter metal), so the rank
reads at a glance without the name.

### B. Pace

The Merit needed per rank grows along a curve (provisional, in `src/config.ts`):

| Stage | Ranks | Merit to reach (first level) | Rough pace for a regular player (~80 Merit a session) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting out | Blank Page to Dog-Ear | 0, 67, 161 | Several promotions in the first sessions |
| Settling in | Paper Dart to Inkslinger | 294, 481, 744 | A promotion most sessions |
| Regulars | Hall Monitor to Origami Ace | 1,113, 1,632, 2,361 | A promotion every few sessions |
| Stars | Sky Scholar to High Flyer | 3,386, 5,424, 8,632 | A promotion every week or two of regular play |
| Veterans | Valedictorian and Dean's List | 13,680, 19,287 | Months |
| The top | Head of the Class (III at 34,117) | 27,164 | Only the most dedicated players |
| Beyond | Honor Roll stars | 4,000 per star | A fixed Merit amount per star, forever |

The step from one level to the next starts at 20 Merit and grows by 12% a level (`MERIT.curve` in
`src/config.ts`).

The developer approves the curve. The economy simulator (v0.0.18) later checks it against the
real reward rates, and the curve can be retuned without anyone losing a rank.

### C. Rules

- **Ranks never go down.** There is no decay and nothing a player does loses Merit.
- **Merit cannot be spent, traded or transferred.** It only counts.
- Ranks belong to the player's identity (the v0.0.10 device token and transfer codes), and move to
  their account when accounts arrive (v0.0.26).

## 2. Merit

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** Merit that rewards real play, that the server can trust, and that cannot be farmed.

### A. Sources

All Merit is decided and recorded by the server, from its own records. Values are provisional.

| Source | Merit |
|---|---|
| A mission completed | By mission tier and difficulty, like paper rewards |
| A shoot-down or an assist on a player | Medium, with the v0.0.9 assist rules |
| A shoot-down of an AI plane | Small, capped per session |
| A raid completed as attacker, or defended | Medium to large |
| An interception | Medium |
| Taking part in a session | Small, for active time only, capped per session |
| End-of-session placement | A bonus for the top places |
| The first session of the day | A one-off bonus, to reward coming back rather than grinding |
| An overshoot kill (v0.0.16) | A small style bonus |
| Medals and achievements | From v0.0.18, when they move to the server |

- Merit is earned in multiplayer only, where the server can verify it. Single-player still shows
  the player's profile card and rank.
- Losing a plane, a raid or a fight never costs Merit.

### B. Anti-farming

- **Diminishing returns** on shooting down the same player repeatedly in a session, and on
  repeated raids between the same two players.
- Shoot-downs of players under new-player protection give reduced Merit.
- Session participation counts only active time: an idle plane on the pad earns nothing.
- From v0.0.19, nothing from session allies, and from v0.0.23, nothing between squad members, in
  line with their anti-farming rules.
- Daily and per-session caps for the small, repeatable sources.

### C. The ledger

- Every Merit award is a row in a **Merit ledger** with an idempotent event id, the same way paper
  works (v0.0.12). A retry, a reconnect or a restart can never award the same thing twice.
- The total and the rank are derived from the ledger, never stored only as a number.
- A new migration adds the ledger and the player's chosen title, card and effects. Earlier
  migrations stay untouched (they are checksummed).
- **Backfill:** players from the alpha already have saved progress (sorties, missions, kills since
  v0.0.12). A one-off, idempotent backfill awards Merit for it, so early testers start at a rank
  that reflects what they have played.
- The admin endpoint (v0.0.14) shows a player's Merit history, and the dashboard shows it in
  v0.0.27.

## 3. Rank Rewards

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** Every tier unlocks something worth showing off, and none of it affects play.

### A. What each tier unlocks

Provisional; the art direction follows the paper-and-pencil style of v0.0.15 and the game's art
direction.

As built (`RANKS` in `src/ranks/Ranks.ts`):

| Rank | Profile card | Plane and desk | Title |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blank Page | Lined notebook paper | The insignia next to the name | "New Sheet" |
| Scribbler | Pencil-smudged border | | "Margin Doodler" |
| Dog-Ear | A dog-eared corner | A desk pennant | "Corner Folder" |
| Paper Dart | Brown kraft paper | A nameplate stripe in the player's colour | "Paper Cut" |
| Glider | Graph paper | **Pencil trail** behind maneuvers and boosts | "Updraft" |
| Inkslinger | Ink splats | Ink-drop trail | "Ink Blot" |
| Hall Monitor | A sash ribbon | A desk banner in the player's colour | "Teacher's Pet" |
| Paper Hawk | Embossed card stock | Feather-fold trail; a kill-feed badge | "Talon" |
| Origami Ace | Origami-patterned card | **Unfold:** a burst of folds as the plane leaves the pad | "The Unfoldable" |
| Sky Scholar, Hawk Eye, High Flyer | Blueprint paper | Constellation sparkle trail (Sky Scholar) | "Top Marks", "Eagle Eye", "Recess Legend" |
| Valedictorian, Dean's List | Gold-foil edges | **Aura:** a faint shimmer around the plane | "Class Speaker", "Honours" |
| Head of the Class | Gilded, gently animated card | Gold leaf in the aura; a gold name in the kill feed | "Head of the Class" |
| Honor Roll | A new card variant every five stars | | "Honor Roll" |

The extra emote at Inkslinger is left for v0.0.23's cosmetics (emotes are a fixed set the server
checks today).

- Each tier also adds titles to choose from (for example "The Unfoldable", "Teacher's Pet",
  "Paper Cut", "Recess Legend"). The player picks one title to show.
- Unlocks stay unlocked. A player can choose an earlier card, trail or title, or turn effects off
  for their own plane.

### B. The cosmetic-only rule

Absolute, and enforced by tests like v0.0.23's customization:

- No rank reward changes speed, handling, damage, hit size, range, ammunition, payload, detection,
  prices, rewards or production.
- Effects never change the plane's silhouette or the friend and foe colours, and never cover
  another plane, a marker or a target.
- **Stealth:** while a plane is hidden by stealth (v0.0.18), its trail and aura are hidden too.
  They can neither reveal a stealth plane nor make one harder to see.
- Effects respect the graphics preset (fewer particles on Low) and a setting: show other
  players' rank effects for **everyone**, **only mine** or **no one**. The setting changes only
  the viewer's screen.

### C. Where it goes later

v0.0.23 builds the full cosmetic inventory. Rank unlocks become inventory items with "Rank" as
their source, alongside achievements and mastery, with no change for players.

## 4. The Profile Card

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** One small card that says who a player is.

### A. Contents

- Username, insignia, rank name and division, and Honor Roll stars.
- The chosen title.
- Progress to the next rank (only on the player's own card).
- Most-flown aircraft, with a small render.
- Three stats the player chooses to show (for example sorties, shoot-downs, raids defended,
  overshoots, sessions played).
- "Flying since" (the month the player started).
- The card's paper and frame from their rank.

No real name, location or anything outside the game ever appears on a card.

### B. Where it appears

| Place | How |
|---|---|
| The scoreboard | The insignia on every row; select a row to open the card |
| Desk nameplates | "{username}'s desk" with the insignia; focus it to open the card |
| Over planes in flight | The insignia next to the name tag, small |
| After being shot down | The card of the player who did it, briefly |
| Session results | The top players' cards |
| The player's own profile | From the home dashboard: the full card, rank progress, unlocks and the equip screen |
| The lobby and main menu | From v0.0.24, built on the same card component |

Every place works with mouse, keyboard and controller focus (v0.0.15's focus model).

## 5. Promotion

**Priority:** High
**Goal:** A rank-up that feels like an event, at the right moment.

- **Never mid-fight.** A promotion earned in flight shows only a small note ("Promoted! See you at
  the desk"). The ceremony waits until the player is back at their desk or on the results screen.
- **The ceremony:** the old insignia is stamped over with the new one ("PROMOTED" in rubber-stamp
  style), the new unlocks are shown once each, and the player can equip them straight away. Under
  five seconds and skippable. First sounds here; the fanfare is finished in v0.0.25.
- **Tier promotions** (a new rank name, not just a division) get a bigger version, and the session
  sees it: a kill-feed line ("Ari is now a Paper Hawk") and the new effects right away.
- Several promotions at once (after the backfill, for example) show as one ceremony that runs
  through them quickly.

---

## 6. Multiplayer and the Server

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** Everyone in a session sees the same ranks and effects, cheaply.

- Each player's rank, title and equipped effects are part of the player state the server sends
  when they join and when they change. That is a few bytes, not per frame.
- The server validates what a player equips against what they have unlocked. A modified client
  cannot show a rank or effect it has not earned.
- Effects are drawn by each client from these few values, so they cost no bandwidth in flight.
- The load test confirms that 16 players with every effect on stay within the v0.0.14 frame and
  bandwidth numbers.

## 7. Testing and Verification

### A. Automated

- The rank curve: Merit to rank and division, the top rank, Honor Roll stars, and a retune that
  never lowers anyone's rank.
- The ledger: idempotent awards across retries, reconnects and restarts; the total always equals
  the sum of the ledger.
- Every Merit source, with its caps and diminishing returns; no Merit from idle time, protected
  players beyond the reduced amount, or single-player.
- The backfill gives the same result when run twice.
- Rank rewards have no gameplay effect (one test per effect, per aircraft), and effects are hidden
  while stealthed.
- The server refuses equipping unearned items.
- The new migration applies cleanly on a copy of the live database (the v0.0.14 restore drill).
- All earlier tests pass.

### B. Browser

- Screenshots of every tier's card, insignia and effects, on the High and Low presets.
- The profile card opened from every place in section 4B, with mouse and controller.
- The promotion ceremony, including several promotions at once.

### C. Playtest

- The developer and the friend play several sessions online and watch their ranks move.
- Questions: Do you notice other players' ranks? Does the climb feel fast enough at the start and
  meaningful later? Did any effect ever get in the way of seeing a plane?

## 11. Definition of Done

- [x] The 15 ranks, their levels and Honor Roll stars work, with the approved names (one name, Dean's List, still provisional).
- [x] Merit is awarded on the server from every listed source, through an idempotent ledger (overshoot kills wait for v0.0.18's overshoot detection; medals for v0.0.18).
- [x] Caps and anti-farming rules are enforced and tested.
- [x] Alpha players are backfilled once, idempotently (on their first join; partial credit, capped at 2,500 Merit: the developer's call).
- [x] Every tier's rewards unlock, can be equipped and stay unlocked.
- [ ] Automated tests prove rank rewards have no gameplay effect and respect stealth (no rule reads a rank, and the effects are drawn only with the plane; the stealth test arrives with stealth in v0.0.18).
- [ ] The profile card appears in every listed place, with mouse, keyboard and controller (built: the player's own card from the desk badge, P or D-pad up; the killer's card after a shoot-down; the top players' cards on the session results; insignia on the scoreboard, name tags, desk nameplates, the kill feed and the results. Not yet: opening another player's full card from a scoreboard row, which is a hold-to-view panel today).
- [x] Promotions never interrupt a fight; the ceremony and session announcements work.
- [x] Every player in a session sees the same ranks and effects; unearned items are refused.
- [ ] Performance stays within the v0.0.14 numbers with every effect on (the effects are one ribbon and one sphere per plane, made only for planes that have them; not yet measured under the load test).
- [ ] All earlier automated tests pass.
- [ ] The developer and the friend have played online and approved the pace.

## Final Design Principle

A rank should tell everyone in the classroom how far a player has come, and make that player a
little proud every time they see their name. It should never tell anyone who will win.
