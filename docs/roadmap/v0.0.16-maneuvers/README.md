# v0.0.16 maneuvers: brainstorm and diagrams (2026-11-06)

The developer's brief for v0.0.16, which replaces the original "Trick + direction" plan where they
differ:

1. Fighters, multiroles and recon planes have maneuvers. Bombers (Widewing, Lancer, Ghost) do not.
2. Two kinds: **automatic** and **manual**.
3. Automatic maneuvers are triggered like the boost, on their own key, and fly a scripted move.
4. Each plane has a set of applicable automatic maneuvers; a maneuver can be on several planes.
5. A plane has **one maneuver slot**, and only the maneuver in that slot can be flown.
6. The slot is chosen **before each sortie** (at the pad, with the rearm), not when the plane is
   folded: fly with maneuver A, land, rearm, switch to B, fly again.
7. Manual maneuvers have no script. They come out of what the player does with a flight model that
   allows them: braking hard, pointing the nose up and slowing right down so the enemy passes,
   pulling through a loop, and so on.

Diagrams: `sheet_automatic.png`, `sheet_manual.png` and one image per move. Each shows the plane at
numbered frames (lighter = earlier) along its dashed path; side views add a strip with the plane's
attitude at each frame. They are generated by `tools/maneuver-diagrams.py` from body-rate scripts
(pitch, roll and yaw rates, speed and "grip") of the kind the game's maneuver tracks use.

## Automatic maneuvers (one in the slot, on the Maneuver key)

| # | Maneuver | Inspired by | What it does | Use | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Crane Flip** | Kulbit | Air-brakes and flips the nose a full turn while sliding forward, exits slow on the same heading | The chaser overshoots; you are behind it | ~1.4 s |
| A2 | **Fold Back** | Immelmann | Half loop up, roll upright: reversed, higher, a bit slower | Turn round on a chaser or a target | ~2 s |
| A3 | **Dive Fold** | Split-S | Half roll, half loop down: reversed, lower, faster | Reverse while escaping, dive on someone below | ~2 s |
| A4 | **Slip Roll** | Barrel roll + sideways shove | A fast roll with a boost that pushes the plane ~2 wingspans left or right (the side from A/D, or the current bank) | Spoils aim; homing rockets re-acquire | ~1 s |
| A5 | **Updraft Roll** | Pop-up attack | Boosted climb with a full roll on the way up, levels off higher on the same heading | Gain height fast, dodge, set up a dive | ~2.5 s |
| A6 | **Paper Hang** | Hammerhead / stall turn | Straight up, hangs, pivots on a wingtip, drops back down the way it came | Reversal in tight spaces; bait | ~3.5 s |
| A7 | **Spiral Drill** | Rolling dive | Corkscrew dive, comes out lower and faster | Escape downward, shake a tail | ~2.5 s |
| A8 | **Paper Pivot** | Flat 180° / drift | Swings the tail round flat, drifting sideways, then boosts out the other way | Level reversal with no height change | ~1.8 s |

Rules (provisional, tuned in playtests): a cooldown like the boost (per move and plane), a minimum
entry speed for the big moves, a clearance check so a move never flies the plane into a desk, no
invulnerability, and steering ignored for the move's length with the aim rewritten to the exit
heading so mouse-aim does not swing the plane back.

**Key:** Left Alt (with the browser default suppressed). Left Ctrl is risky: Ctrl+W closes the tab
in most browsers, and W is the throttle. Controller: A / Cross, which is free in flight.

## Manual maneuvers (no script: what the flight model allows)

| # | Maneuver | How | Needs from the flight model |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Brake and Let Pass** | Hold S hard and pull up; the plane bleeds down to air-brake speed, the chaser flies past; nose back onto it and boost | An **air brake** below the normal minimum speed while S is held; a boost that kicks hard from low speed |
| M2 | **Full Loop / Flip** | Keep pulling back: up, over the top inverted, round again | Pitch **through vertical** (no 60° limit), orientation that works upside down |
| M3 | **High Yo-Yo** | Too fast behind a turner: climb out of their turn, roll over, drop back in | Climbing trades speed for height, diving gives it back (stronger energy effect) |
| M4 | **Low Yo-Yo** | Too slow: dive inside their turn to gain speed and cut the corner | Same energy trade |
| M5 | **Scissors** | Weave across each other; whoever is slower ends up behind | Air brake; quick reversals |
| M6 | **Break Turn** | Brake while turning for a much tighter circle | **Speed-dependent turn rate**: slower (down to a "corner speed") turns tighter |
| M7 | **Boom and Zoom** | Dive with speed, shoot, climb back out of reach on that speed | Energy trade; dive speed above the normal top speed |
