# paperwars.io: Version 0.0.16 Development Plan

**Development stage:** Private Playable Alpha
**Release type:** Gameplay feature
**Release theme:** Flight Maneuvers: big, readable dogfight moves on one button and one direction
**Builds on:** [v0.0.15](v0.0.15.md) (HUD and UI revamp), [v0.0.14](v0.0.14.md) (going live) and [v0.0.11](v0.0.11.md) (art integration and controller support)
**Followed by:** [v0.0.17](v0.0.17.md) (player ranks)

Part of the [roadmap to v0.1.0](README.md). Added on 2026-11-06 (see
[the decisions](README.md#decisions-of-2026-11-06)).

---

## Overview

Flight today is mouse-aim arcade flight (`src/aircraft/FlightModel.ts`): the controls move an aim
direction, the plane banks into a turn and the heading follows the bank. There are no stalls and
the plane goes where it is pointed. It is easy to fly, but a dogfight is mostly two planes turning
in a circle. Whoever has the better turn rate wins, and nobody can do anything surprising.

Version 0.0.16 adds **maneuvers**: short, scripted moves that look spectacular and change a fight.
A fast fighter brakes hard, flips nose-over-tail in the air and lets the chaser fly straight past,
so the hunted plane ends up behind the hunter. The Su-57's flip in *Top Gun: Maverick* is the
feeling to aim for. The physics are not meant to be realistic. They are meant to be fun, fair and
easy to read, for the pilot doing the move and for everyone watching it.

**One button and one direction.** Every maneuver is the **Trick** button plus the direction the
player is already pushing. There are no combos to memorise, no timing windows and no quarter
circles. A player who knows "Trick + brake = flip" knows the most important move in the game.

The release has six central objectives:

1. Five maneuvers, each on Trick plus a direction, readable from the outside and with a clear use
   in a fight.
2. A deterministic maneuver system in the shared flight model, so the player's game, the server's
   prediction replay and the AI all fly the same move.
3. A maneuver set per aircraft: fighters get the full set and the sharpest flips, bombers get a
   few slow, heavy moves. The planes play more differently.
4. A maneuver camera, effects and feedback that make each move feel huge without making anyone
   seasick.
5. Teaching that needs no manual: a trick card in the garage, a short flight-school chapter, and a
   contextual prompt the first few times a move would save the player.
6. Multiplayer that stays fair: server validation, charges instead of spam, real counterplay and
   no invulnerability.

Preserve:

- The flight model's character outside maneuvers: mouse-aim, auto-level and forgiving low-speed
  flight stay exactly as they are.
- The authoritative server, client prediction and interpolation (v0.0.9 to v0.0.14). A maneuver is
  an input like any other and the server has the final word.
- Every existing feature and its tests.

Do not expand scope. The new HUD elements are built from the v0.0.15 design system. Maneuver
sounds get a first pass here and their final mix in v0.0.18. Rebinding belongs to v0.0.19. Stunt
missions that use maneuvers belong to v0.0.20.

## 1. The Maneuvers

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** A small set of moves that anyone can perform on the first try, and that a good pilot
uses to win fights.

### A. The set

The names are paper-themed (provisional, the developer approves them). "Back" means braking or
pulling the nose up, and "forward" means speeding up or pushing the nose down.

| Input | Maneuver | Inspired by | What it does | Use in a fight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trick + back | **Crane Flip** | Kulbit / Pugachev's Cobra | Brakes hard, flips the nose up and over a full turn while the plane keeps sliding slowly forward, then comes out on the same heading at low speed | The chaser overshoots. You end up behind them, slow, guns pointed at their tail |
| Trick alone | **Fold Back** | Immelmann turn | Half loop upward, then rolls upright: you are flying the other way, higher and a little slower | Reverse direction fast to face a chaser or go back for a target |
| Trick + forward | **Dive Fold** | Split-S | Half roll, then pulls through a downward half loop: the other way, lower and faster | Reverse direction while escaping, or dive onto someone below |
| Trick + left / right | **Corkscrew** | Barrel roll | A fast roll around the flight path that shifts the plane sideways by about two wingspans | Spoils a chaser's lead on their shots and makes homing rockets re-acquire |
| Trick + back while boosting | **Paper Hang** (fighters only) | Hammerhead / stall turn | Climbs straight up, hangs for a moment at the top, pivots and drops back down the way it came | A showy reversal in tight spaces, and bait: a hanging plane looks easy |

That is five moves on four directions. The fifth, Paper Hang, is a variation of the Crane Flip
that fighters get when they flip during a boost. It is optional; the developer decides at the
phase 2 checkpoint whether it stays or the set is four.

### B. Inputs

| | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Trick | **Space** (it only launches at the desk; it is free in flight, phase 1 confirms it) | **A / Cross** (`south`, free in flight) |
| Back | Holding **S** (brake) | Left stick pulled back |
| Forward | Holding **W** | Left stick pushed forward |
| Left / right | Holding **A** / **D** | Left stick left / right |
| Nothing held | Fold Back | Fold Back |

- The direction is read **at the moment Trick is pressed**, so "hold the brake, tap Trick" and
  "tap Trick while pulling back" both give the Crane Flip. On the controller, the left stick
  direction counts once it is past half deflection. Otherwise the input is "nothing held".
- The mouse does not choose maneuvers. It keeps steering, and a mouse flick never triggers a move
  by accident.
- A maneuver lasts 0.7 to 1.6 seconds (per move and per plane, in `src/config.ts`). Steering is
  ignored while it runs. Firing works during the moves that allow it (section 1C). There is no
  cancel, because the moves are short and the commitment is part of the counterplay.
- **Chaining:** pressing Trick again in the last 30 % of a move queues the next one, so a Crane
  Flip can roll straight into a Corkscrew. It costs a second charge (section 1D).
- The bindings are listed in `src/input/Controls.ts` (a new `trick` action) and become rebindable
  with everything else in v0.0.19.

### C. What each move allows

| Maneuver | Guns | Bombs | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crane Flip | During the "nose-through" window, where the nose passes level (the **flip shot**) | No | Comes out at minimum speed. The boost is available straight after |
| Fold Back | Yes, from the top of the loop on | No | |
| Dive Fold | Yes, in the pull-out | Yes, released at the bottom (a dive-bombing trick) | |
| Corkscrew | No | No | |
| Paper Hang | At the pivot | No | |

### D. Charges, speed and space

Maneuvers are limited so they stay special:

- **Crease charges:** each plane has 1 to 3 charges (section 3). A move uses one, and each charge
  folds back in after a few seconds (provisional: 6 s for fighters, up to 10 s for bombers). With
  no charge left, Trick does nothing and the HUD shows why.
- **Entry speed:** every move needs a minimum speed, as a fraction between the plane's minimum and
  maximum speed. The boost counts. Provisional values: Crane Flip 70 %, Paper Hang 70 % while
  boosting, Fold Back 40 %, Dive Fold 25 %, Corkscrew any speed. "Going fast" is part of the move,
  as the developer described it.
- **Space:** the move only starts if its path is clear. Dive Fold needs height above whatever is
  below; Fold Back and Paper Hang need clearance above. The check sweeps the move's path against
  the same colliders the flight model uses. If the path is blocked, Trick does nothing and the HUD
  says "Too low" or "No room" instead of flying the player into a desk.
- **Low health:** a badly damaged plane (below the v0.0.11 wobble threshold) flies its moves
  slower and wider, like its normal handling.
- **Gliding** (out of range): no maneuvers except Corkscrew.

## 2. The Maneuver System in the Flight Model

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** Maneuvers that behave identically in the pilot's game, the server and the AI, and that
survive prediction, reconnection and snapshots.

### A. Authored tracks

Each maneuver is an **authored track**: curves over normalised time (0 to 1) for

- the nose's rotation relative to the entry frame (pitch, yaw and roll offsets),
- the speed, as a multiplier of the entry speed,
- the **grip**: how much the velocity follows the nose. Low grip is what makes the Crane Flip look
  right: the nose spins over while the plane keeps sliding along its old path.

At the start, the model records an entry frame (position, heading, pitch, speed). Every step it
samples the track and sets the orientation and the velocity from it, then runs the normal
collision step. Tracks are data (`src/aircraft/maneuvers.ts` plus per-plane scales in
`src/config.ts`). They are not physics, so every machine flies them identically for the same
inputs.

### B. Orientation through vertical

The flight model stores yaw, pitch and roll as angles, which break down at pitch ±90°, and the
Crane Flip, Fold Back and Paper Hang all pass through vertical. While a maneuver runs, orientation
is a quaternion composed on the entry frame. When the move ends, it converts back to yaw, pitch
and roll, which is always possible because every track ends in level-ish flight. Tests cover every
move at every entry heading and bank.

### C. Exit

When a move ends:

- the **aim direction is rewritten to the new heading.** With mouse-aim, the plane otherwise
  steers straight back to where the mouse was pointing before the reversal. This is the most
  important detail in the whole system.
- the throttle setting is set to the exit speed, so the plane does not surge or stall back to the
  old throttle.
- auto-level and normal steering blend back in over about 0.2 s.

### D. Collisions and damage during a move

- Collisions work normally. Hitting something ends the move and uses the normal impact rules.
- Hits from guns and rockets land normally. **There is no invulnerability at any point.** The
  hit sphere does not care about orientation, so nothing about hit detection changes.
- If the plane is shot down mid-move, it crashes from where it is.

### E. Snapshots and determinism

- The `FlightSnapshot` (`FLIGHT_SNAPSHOT_SIZE`, 23 today) grows by the maneuver id, its elapsed
  time, the queued follow-up, the charges and the recharge timer. The prediction tests that rewind
  and replay (v0.0.9) cover a move started, chained and interrupted by a collision.
- The flight model's existing rule holds: the same snapshot plus the same inputs give the same
  result on the pilot's game and the server.

## 3. Maneuver Sets per Aircraft

**Priority:** High
**Goal:** Each plane's set says something about it: fighters are acrobats, bombers are heavy.

Provisional, for the developer to approve. A ✓ means the move is available. Durations and the size
of the moves scale with the plane's existing turn and pitch rates, so a Falcon's flip is snappier
than a Swift's without extra tuning.

| Aircraft | Role | Fold Back | Dive Fold | Corkscrew | Crane Flip | Paper Hang | Charges |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Falcon | Agile fighter | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (fastest) | ✓ | 3 |
| Raptor | Air superiority | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (thrust-vectored: comes out faster) | ✓ | 3 |
| Lightning | Stealth multirole | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | – | 2 |
| Jumper | VTOL fighter | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (the tightest flip: barely moves forward) | – | 2 |
| Swift | Starter / basic fighter | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (slow and wide, so new players learn it) | – | 2 |
| Thug | Ground attack | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | – | – | 2 |
| Dart | Fast recon | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | – | – | 2 |
| Specter | Surveillance drone | ✓ | – | ✓ | – | – | 1 |
| Lancer | Supersonic bomber | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (slow) | – | – | 1 |
| Widewing | Bomber | ✓ (wide) | – | ✓ (slow) | – | – | 1 |
| Ghost | Stealth bomber | – | – | ✓ (a gentle sideslip) | – | – | 1 |

- The Swift gets the Crane Flip on purpose: the starter plane should teach the signature move. A
  Swift's flip is slow enough that a Falcon pilot can punish a badly timed one.
- The Wasp and Hawk helicopters (v0.0.24) get their own hover moves in that version.
- The garage card shows the plane's set (section 5A).

## 4. Camera, Effects and Feel

**Priority:** High
**Goal:** Every move feels huge for the pilot and reads clearly for everyone else.

### A. The maneuver camera

The chase camera (`src/aircraft/ChaseCamera.ts`) does not follow the plane through a flip, which
would spin the world around the player. During a move it:

- holds a stable outside view with the world's up kept up, and the plane centred,
- pulls back a little at the start of a Crane Flip, so the pilot **sees the chaser fly past**:
  this is the payoff moment and the camera is framed for it,
- swings back behind the plane over the last part of the move, so control returns with the
  camera already behind the new heading,
- with reduced motion turned on, stays steadier and swings slower.

### B. Effects

- Paper-style wingtip streaks during every move (pencil-line swishes, not realistic vapour).
- A crinkle of the paper at the start of the flip and a quick flex of the wings at the pull-out.
- A brief "whoosh" doodle near the plane for the Crane Flip, seen by everyone nearby.
- First sounds for each move (a paper snap, a whoosh, wind). The final versions and the mix come in
  v0.0.18.
- No camera shake, no screen flash and no slow motion (time cannot slow down in multiplayer).

### C. The overshoot

The game recognises when a Crane Flip works: an enemy that was behind the pilot within a short
distance passes in front of them during or just after the flip. Then:

- the pilot gets a quick "Overshoot!" marker on the enemy who flew past,
- if the pilot hits that enemy within a few seconds, the kill feed shows a small flip icon next to
  the kill,
- the event is recorded for later use: Merit for player ranks (v0.0.17), medals and stunt missions
  (v0.0.20).

## 5. Learning the Moves

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** A new player does a Crane Flip in their first session without reading anything.

### A. In the garage

Every aircraft card gets a **trick card**: one small doodle per move showing its path, with the
input glyph for the active device (keyboard or controller, through `src/input/glyphs.ts`). Moves
the plane does not have are shown greyed, so players see what a fighter can do.

### B. Flight school

A short chapter added to the v0.0.11 tutorial (single-player and multiplayer, saved like the rest
of the tutorial):

1. "Tap Trick": Fold Back, the easiest move.
2. "Hold the brake and tap Trick": the Crane Flip, with a friendly AI Swift following close
   behind. The chapter ends when the Swift overshoots and the player gets behind it.
3. "Push and tap Trick" and "Steer and tap Trick": Dive Fold and Corkscrew, one try each.

It takes under two minutes, can be skipped, and is offered the first time a player flies a plane
with maneuvers, not forced at the start of the game.

### C. Contextual prompts

- When an enemy is close on the pilot's tail and the Crane Flip is possible, a prompt shows the
  input ("Brake + Space: Crane Flip"). After the player has used the move a few times, the prompt
  shrinks to an icon, and later disappears. A setting turns prompts off.
- When Trick does nothing, the HUD says why: "Too slow", "No charge", "Too low", "No room" or "Not
  on this plane".

### D. HUD

Built from the v0.0.15 design system, in the space it leaves beside the boost meter:

- **Crease charges:** small folded paper corners, filled when ready, with the refill shown.
- A small marker when the plane is fast enough for its best move.
- The refusal reasons and contextual prompts, with device-aware glyphs.

## 6. Multiplayer

**Priority:** Critical
**Goal:** Maneuvers that look smooth on every screen and that the server can trust.

### A. Authority

- The Trick press and its direction go in the input frame like the other controls.
- The server checks the plane's set, charges, speed and clearance, and runs the same track. A move
  the client predicted but the server refused is corrected through the normal snapshot rewind.
- A modified client cannot start moves its plane does not have, skip charges, or change a track.

### B. Other players' planes

- Interpolating fast rotation between snapshots would cut corners through a flip. Instead, the
  server announces the maneuver id and the step it started on, and every client plays the same
  track locally for remote planes, corrected by the normal snapshots.
- Remote orientation is interpolated as a quaternion during moves.
- The bandwidth cost is a few bytes per move start; the load test (v0.0.14) confirms it.

### C. Reconnection and sessions

- A plane in the middle of a move when its pilot disconnects finishes the move and then flies on
  under the v0.0.13 disconnect rules.
- Charges are part of the saved flight state, like the boost.

## 7. AI Pilots

**Priority:** High
**Goal:** The AI uses the moves, and falls for them, at a level that fits the mission difficulty.

- AI planes fly through the same flight model, so they get maneuvers for free. They only need to
  decide when.
- **Using them:** AI aces (higher director difficulty tiers) use the Crane Flip when a player sits
  on their tail, the Corkscrew against rockets, and Fold Back to re-engage. Easy and newcomer
  missions (GDD §11.3) use none, or only Fold Back.
- **Falling for them:** AI chasers react to a Crane Flip with a reaction delay set by difficulty,
  so a well-timed flip makes easy AI overshoot every time and an ace sometimes.
- Defenses (turrets, rocket launchers) are not changed, but their lead prediction naturally misses
  a Corkscrewing plane. That is intended.

## 8. Balance and Counterplay

**Priority:** High
**Goal:** Maneuvers win fights for pilots who use them well, and lose fights for pilots who use
them badly.

- **The Crane Flip is a gamble.** If the chaser was too far away or braked as well, the flipper is
  now slow, close and easy to hit. The best answer to a flip is to brake or pull up instead of
  flying past.
- **Fold Back and Dive Fold are predictable.** The path is fixed for the length of the move, so a
  good shot can lead it.
- **The Corkscrew does not make anyone invulnerable.** It only spoils aim and makes homing rockets
  re-acquire (about half a second, provisional).
- **Charges limit spam,** and bombers have one.
- **No move adds damage.** The flip shot is ordinary gunfire.
- The values start in `src/config.ts` as provisional and are tuned from the online playtests and
  the F8 feedback log (v0.0.14), the same process as the PvP values.

## 9. Testing and Verification

### A. Automated

- Every move's track: start and end orientation, exit heading, exit speed and path length, for
  every aircraft, at several entry headings, pitches and banks, including through vertical.
- The exit rewrites the aim, so the plane does not turn back after a reversal.
- Entry rules: speed, charges, clearance, gliding, the plane's set, low health.
- Chaining, the 30 % window and the charge cost.
- Collisions during a move end it and apply normal damage. Hits land during every move.
- Snapshot save and restore mid-move, and prediction replay: client and server agree step by step.
- The server refuses invalid moves (wrong plane, no charge, too slow, blocked).
- Overshoot detection: a scripted chaser passes and is detected; near misses that should not count
  are not.
- AI: easy AI never uses the Crane Flip, aces do, and reaction delays are applied.
- All earlier tests pass.

### B. Browser

- Headless Chromium runs of each move for keyboard and a simulated controller, with screenshots
  at key frames, in single-player and multiplayer (two bots, one chasing the other).
- Remote planes' moves look the same as the pilot's own, compared frame by frame against the
  server.
- Frame time is unchanged within noise with several planes doing moves at once.

### C. Hands-on

- The developer and the friend dogfight online with only the maneuvers' first tuning, then again
  after a tuning pass.
- Questions: Can you do each move on the first try? Do you know which one you did? Did a flip ever
  feel unfair from the receiving end? Did the camera ever make you lose track of where you were?

## 12. AI Agent Implementation Strategy

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Audit the flight model, camera, input layer and snapshot; confirm Space and A / Cross are free in flight; the `trick` action; the regression baseline |
| 2 | The track system, quaternion orientation, exit handling; Crane Flip and Fold Back flying in single-player. **Checkpoint:** the developer flies them |
| 3 | Dive Fold, Corkscrew, Paper Hang, chaining; per-aircraft sets, charges, entry rules and clearance checks |
| 4 | The maneuver camera, effects and first sounds; overshoot detection and the kill-feed icon |
| 5 | Multiplayer: input frames, server validation, snapshots, prediction replay, remote playback; bots and the load test |
| 6 | AI pilots: using the moves and reacting to them, by difficulty |
| 7 | Garage trick cards, the flight-school chapter, contextual prompts and the HUD elements. **Checkpoint:** the online dogfight playtest and the tuning pass |

- Work in reviewable increments, each with screenshots or short captures at a developer checkpoint.
- Do not mark a phase complete because the code compiles; verify it with tests, browser runs and
  play.
- Preserve every working feature from earlier versions.
- Record decisions and open questions in the README.

## 13. Definition of Done

- [ ] Every move works on Trick plus a direction, on keyboard and on controller.
- [ ] Each aircraft has its approved set and charges, shown on its garage card.
- [ ] Moves pass through vertical cleanly, end level, and never turn back after a reversal.
- [ ] Entry rules (speed, charges, clearance, gliding) work and the HUD says why a move is refused.
- [ ] No move gives invulnerability or extra damage; collisions and hits work during every move.
- [ ] The pilot's game and the server agree step by step, including chained and interrupted moves.
- [ ] Other players' moves look smooth and identical on every screen.
- [ ] The AI uses moves by difficulty and can be overshot.
- [ ] The camera keeps the player oriented, including with reduced motion.
- [ ] The flight-school chapter and contextual prompts teach the moves; a first-time player does a
      Crane Flip without help.
- [ ] Frame time and bandwidth stay within the v0.0.14 numbers.
- [ ] All earlier automated tests pass.
- [ ] The developer and the friend have dogfought online with maneuvers and approved the tuning.

---

## Final Design Principle

A maneuver should take one second to learn, one session to use well, and much longer to master.
Anyone can flip; the pilot who flips at the right moment wins.
