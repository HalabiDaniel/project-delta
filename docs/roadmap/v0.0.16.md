## The developer's brief (2026-11-06)

1. Fighters, multiroles and recon planes have maneuvers. Slow planes (the bombers) do not.
2. There are two kinds: **automatic** and **manual**.
3. Automatic maneuvers work like the boost: one key flies a scripted move (an upward boost then a
   roll, a barrel roll to the left or right with a sideways boost, and so on). There are several.
4. Each plane has a set of maneuvers it can carry, and a maneuver can be on several planes.
5. A plane has **one maneuver slot**, and can only fly the maneuver in it.
6. The maneuver is chosen **before each flight**, not when the plane is made: fly with A, land,
   rearm, change to B, take off and fly B.
7. Manual maneuvers have no script: the player flies them by flying smartly (braking hard, pointing
   up, slowing right down so the enemy passes, then nosing back on and boosting; turning on into a
   flip). The flight model has to allow them.

## 1. Automatic maneuvers

**Status: done.** Eight moves, as authored tracks (`src/aircraft/maneuvers.ts`), tuned in
`src/config.ts` (`MANEUVERS`, `MANEUVER`).

| Maneuver | Inspired by | What it does | Length* | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Crane Flip** | Kulbit | Air-brakes and flips the nose a full turn while sliding on; out slow, same heading. The chaser overshoots | 1.4 s | 7 s |
| **Fold Back** | Immelmann | Half loop up, roll upright: reversed, ~11 m higher, a little slower | 1.9 s | 6 s |
| **Dive Fold** | Split-S | Half roll, half loop down: reversed, ~15 m lower, faster | 1.9 s | 6 s |
| **Slip Roll** | Barrel roll | A full roll with a sideways shove of ~6 m, towards A or D (else the current bank, else left) | 1.0 s | 4.5 s |
| **Updraft Roll** | Pop-up attack | Boosted climb with a full roll, levels off ~15 m higher on the same heading | 2.2 s | 6 s |
| **Paper Hang** | Hammerhead | Straight up, hangs, pivots on a wingtip, back down the way it came | 3.2 s | 8 s |
| **Spiral Drill** | Rolling dive | Corkscrew dive (two rolls), out lower and faster on the same heading | 2.2 s | 6 s |
| **Paper Pivot** | Flat 180° | The tail swings round flat while the plane drifts, then it boosts out the other way | 1.6 s | 7 s |

\* On a plane with turn rate 1.6; nimbler planes fly them faster, damaged ones slower.

**Keys:** **Left Alt** (the browser's menu-bar default is suppressed) and **A / Cross** in flight.
Left Ctrl was considered and rejected: Ctrl+W closes the tab, and W is the throttle.

**Rules:**

- A cooldown per move, like the boost; the HUD bar refills.
- A minimum entry speed for the big moves (`minEntry`, a share of the way from minimum to top speed).
- A **clearance check**: the move's path is flown ahead of time (no collisions) and must keep clear
  of desks and props and stay above the classroom floor; otherwise the key does nothing and the HUD
  says "Too low" or "No room".
- Out of range (gliding) only the Slip Roll works. Steering is ignored for the move's length.
- No invulnerability and no extra damage: hits and collisions work as always, and hitting
  something ends the move with the normal impact rules.
- At the end the aim is rewritten to the new heading (so mouse-aim does not swing the plane back
  after a reversal) and the throttle to the exit speed.

## 2. Manual maneuvers: what the flight model allows

**Status: done** (`src/aircraft/FlightModel.ts`, tuned in `ACRO`):

- **Air brake:** holding S with the throttle at the bottom slows the plane to 45 % of its minimum
  speed; below the minimum it mushes down a little. The boost kicks back hard from there.
- **Slower turns tighter:** up to 55 % more turn and pitch rate at the air-brake floor (none at
  cruise and above): braking in a turn out-turns a plane at full speed.
- **Loops:** pulling (or pushing) on past the 60° pitch limit lifts it, for fighters, multiroles and
  recon planes. The nose turns in the plane's own frame through vertical and upside down; A/D roll.
  Let go and the plane carries on until its nose is back within the limit, rolls itself upright and
  hands back to normal flight. Bombers keep the limit.

Together they give the moves of the [diagrams](v0.0.16-maneuvers/README.md): brake and let pass,
full loop, high and low yo-yo, scissors, break turn, boom and zoom.

## 4. The slot, chosen before each flight

**Status: done.** `GameState.maneuverChoice` remembers the choice per design (saved: `SaveData.maneuvers`
in single-player, the player's garage record in multiplayer). It can be changed at home (at the
desk, on the pad, and stopped after landing) with **T**, the right stick click (**RS / R3**) or the
picker's arrows; it is locked in the air ("Chosen before take-off: land at T1 to change it").

## 5. HUD, camera and sound

**Status: done.** Built from the v0.0.15 components:

- The **maneuver slot** beside the boost: the move's name, its readiness bar, its key, and a note:
  why a press did nothing, "Air brake", or "Looping".
- The **picker** card at home (bottom centre): ‹ name › with "2/6" and the key.
- The **chase camera** follows the heading of travel during a move or a loop (level, a little further
  back, world-up kept up, the plane centred), so a flip never spins the world; it swings back behind
  the plane as normal flight returns.
- The existing whoosh plays at the start of a move. Final sounds are v0.0.25.

## 6. Multiplayer

**Status: done.**

- The Maneuver press goes in the input frame (flag bit 256); the server flies the same track.
- The slot is the server's: a `maneuver` command, checked against the plane's set and refused in
  the air. The server sets the flight model's slot from it every step; a modified client cannot
  fly a move its plane lacks or skip a cooldown (both are in the authoritative flight state).
- The flight snapshot (`FLIGHT_SNAPSHOT_SIZE` 23 → 39) carries the slot, the move in progress, the
  travel direction, the cooldown and the loop state, so prediction and reconciliation replay a move
  exactly.
- Remote planes and the pilot's own smoothed pose interpolate the attitude as a quaternion (slerp),
  so a flip through vertical draws cleanly.

## 7. AI pilots

**Status: done (first pass).** `AI.skill.*.maneuvers` and `knows`: rookies never fly one; regulars
sometimes fly a Fold Back or Slip Roll when chased; veterans carry the best move their plane has
(Crane Flip first) and use it more often. The AI's turrets naturally miss a Slip Roll.

- `src/aircraft/maneuvers.test.ts`: sets (bombers none), every move on every plane that carries it

## Final design principle

A maneuver should take one second to learn, one session to use well, and much longer to master.
The automatic ones are the spectacle; the manual ones are the skill.
