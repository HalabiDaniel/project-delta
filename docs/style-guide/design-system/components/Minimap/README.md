# Minimap

A round game minimap of the classroom, in the HUD's bottom-right corner.

It shows the floor and its planks, the chalkboard and teacher's desk at the top, every desk with its number and chair, and the pencils and erasers lying around. Claimed desks have a dashed territory zone in their team colour; yours is `sky-500`. You are the white paper-plane arrow, with a blue view cone; outside your vision radius the map is under fog. Allies are blue dots, enemy raiders are red triangles, and the mission target is a pencil-yellow star in a dashed ring, so raids and goals read by shape as well as colour. The rim has a compass `N`, eight ticks, the desk you are over and zoom buttons (Lucide `plus` and `minus`). Give the map a text label that says where you are, where the target is and where the raiders are, for screen readers. The game draws the live map; the consumer keeps the frame, colours and markers from here.
